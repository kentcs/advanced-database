Add a Yearly Ratings Summary to Your IMDb Page

Your Chapter 16 Streamlit page finds individual titles. Add a second view that answers a different question: what is the mean rating of qualifying movies in each release year? Keep the title search working.

Build it

  1. Work in a copy of your Chapter 16 app. Add an IMDb ratings by year section or tab, with first year, last year, and minimum votes inputs. Use 1990, 1999, and 1,000 as the starting values.
  2. Add a query helper that sends an aggregation pipeline to db.titles. Match title_type: "movie" and the inclusive year range; join ratings on _id; unwind the rating; require the vote threshold and numeric rating; group by start_year; sort; and project the display columns.
  3. Show year, rated_movies, mean_rating, and total_votes in a table. Add a bar chart of the mean by year. State on the page that each qualifying movie has equal weight. You are averaging title-level average_rating values, not individual votes and not weighting by num_votes.
  4. Keep the computation in MongoDB. Python should submit the pipeline and display its returned rows. Do not fetch all titles and group them in Pandas.
  5. Handle reversed years and no matching movies. Keep credentials in MONGODB_URI, as in Chapter 16. Do not write to or replace the collections.

Use the worked query helper and standalone demonstration if you get stuck. Your submission should extend your Chapter 16 app, not replace its title search.

Check your work

First predict the tiny example in the primer: at 1,000 votes, the 1990 row has 2 movies, mean 7.0, and 3,000 votes; 1991 has 1 movie, mean 7.5, and 1,500 votes. At 1,500 votes, the 1990 row becomes 1 movie, mean 8.0, and 2,000 votes. The supplied tests check this on an isolated server; do not seed these invented movies into your course database.

Then run your page on the Chapter 16 IMDb data. Submit your app and helper, a screenshot of the table and chart, and a short explanation of what changed when you raised the minimum votes from 1,000 to 10,000. Include the year range and dataset you used. Explain why a changed mean does not establish a trend in movie quality. Confirm that title search still works.
